# Where NaPTAN and the override file disagree about a station's name

Generated by stripping the `Rail Station` suffix from NaPTAN's `CommonName` and comparing it
against `libs/gtfs/src/data/station-coordinates.ts`. It is a review document, not an assertion:
it says where the two differ, not which is right.

D3 declined to take NaPTAN's names because its `CommonName` is "Aberdare Rail Station" where the
departure boards say "Aberdare". That suffix turns out to be the whole of the objection.

**2,580 stations are described by both sources. 2,454 names are identical once the suffix is
gone.** Nine stations have no NaPTAN record at all. The 126 that differ:

| kind | count |
|---|---:|
| a parenthesised qualifier, present in one and not the other or abbreviated differently | 98 |
| case only | 6 |
| genuinely different names | 22 |

This matters for **D7**: retiring `station-coordinates.ts` takes 2,593 readable station names with
it, and nothing else supplies them. NaPTAN covers 95% of them exactly.

## Genuinely different

`Lincoln Central`), some are the override being more specific (`Cark & Cartmel` against `Cark`),
and at least one is NaPTAN being wrong - `Burnham (Berks)`, when Burnham station is in
Buckinghamshire.
